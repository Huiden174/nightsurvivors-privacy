Night Survivors — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Night Survivors (package com.huidenchen.nightsurvivors, "the App") handles information. The App is developed by an independent developer based in Malaysia ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not require an account to play.

  • Game progress (currencies, unlocked items, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation from Google so we can deliver the item; we never see your payment card details.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). Players who buy "Remove Ads" or the Annual Card no longer see banner and interstitial ads.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device. Uninstalling the App, or using "Reset Save Data" in the App's settings, deletes it. To ask about data held by Google for ads, see the Google links above.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
